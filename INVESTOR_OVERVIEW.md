## 1) Business Overview

Execify is a subscription-based, AI-augmented executive operations partner built for India’s startup and SMB ecosystem. We provide founders and business owners with a high-leverage support layer across strategy and operations—without the cost, hiring friction, or management overhead of a full-time senior hire.

### What we do
We handle recurring and ad hoc executive workloads such as:
- Competitive and market intelligence
- Investor materials and narrative development
- Hiring pipeline support
- Vendor and partner coordination
- SOP and process documentation
- Founder-office execution support

### Why now
Global models (e.g., Invisible Technologies, Magic, and other AI-operations firms) have validated premium remote support subscriptions in the US/UK. India’s market is large, fragmented, and underserved—creating a first-mover opportunity for a premium, AI-first service model.

## 2) Problem & Value Proposition

Founders and SMB operators often spend 60–70% of their time on operational execution instead of strategic growth. This leads to slower decisions, inconsistent follow-through, and revenue leakage.

Execify addresses this with:
- **Fast strategic output:** decks, reports, SOPs, and briefs delivered in hours
- **AI-first leverage:** 5–10x execution speed per operator
- **Human quality control:** every output reviewed before delivery
- **Predictable subscription economics:** recurring monthly plans with clear scope and SLAs
- **Non-linear scalability:** standardized workflows and templates reduce delivery effort per client over time

## 4) Standard Operating Workflow (5-Step Delivery System)

Every engagement follows a fixed operating cadence to maximize quality and reliability.

### 1. Client Intake & Onboarding (within 48 hours)
- Discovery call and needs diagnosis
- Priority mapping and KPI definition
- Workspace setup (Notion + communication channels)
- SOP baseline and first-week task backlog

### 2. Task Intake
- Requests received via dedicated Slack channel or WhatsApp Business
- Tasks tagged by category (urgent/important/routine)
- Same-day triage and SLA assignment

### 3. AI-Augmented Execution
- Operator uses LLM and research stack for drafting, analysis, and synthesis
- Standard templates used for repeat task classes
- Context pulled from client workspace to reduce back-and-forth

### 4. Quality Review & Delivery
- Human review for factual accuracy, strategic fit, and tone
- Deliverables sent as polished doc + Loom briefing (where relevant)
- Revision loop closed within SLA

### 5. Monthly Review & Retention Loop
- 30-minute performance review with client
- Output quality and turnaround metrics review
- Priority realignment and upsell/upgrade discussion

## 7) Go-To-Market Plan

### Phase 1: Validation (Month 1–2)
- Focus segment: D2C founders with 5–50 employees
- Offer short pilot engagements to known founders
- Capture testimonials and baseline case outcomes
- Launch founder-facing LinkedIn authority content

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 6+)

## 9) Why India, Why This Moment

- India’s SMB base (80M+) remains largely underserved by structured executive ops support
- AI capability has matured enough to deliver reliable first-draft intelligence at low cost
- International service models have validated willingness to pay for outsourced execution
- Indian founders are increasingly open to async, outcome-based support models
- No dominant, premium, AI-augmented executive support brand currently owns this category
